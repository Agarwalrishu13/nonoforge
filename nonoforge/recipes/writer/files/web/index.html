    What am I actually looking at?
    

      
This is a trained model. Not a shortcut, not a fake: training means
      adjusting numbers so that the model gets better at guessing what comes next, and that is
      exactly what happens when you press Learn. It counts which letters follow which
      other letters in your writing, and how often.

      
When it writes, it looks at the last three letters it produced, finds what usually came
      after those letters in your text, and picks one — usually the most common, sometimes
      a rarer one, depending on the creativity dial.

      
So it will not be clever. It has no idea what words mean. But it is the same trick the big
      models use, with far more context and arithmetic — and you can read every line of this one in
      start.py.

      
Nothing is uploaded. Your text stays in the samples folder next to this app,
      and "Forget what I pasted" deletes the pasted part.
