Put writing of your own in this folder.

Any .txt or .md file works - a story, your emails, a diary, the notes you take.
The more of your own writing it sees, the more the model sounds like you.

You do not have to use this folder at all: you can also paste text straight
into the page and press "Learn from this". That gets saved here too, so the
model keeps what it learnt next time you start the app.

Files whose names start with an underscore or a dot are ignored, which is why
this note is never part of the training text. Delete it whenever you like.
