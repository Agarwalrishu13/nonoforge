Put .txt or .md files into the notes folder next to this app, then press refresh here. Word documents and PDFs can be copied and pasted into Notepad or TextEdit and saved as .txt.

How this works (and what it is not)

It does not understand your question. It breaks your notes into paragraphs, looks for the paragraphs that share the most words with what you typed, and sorts them so the best match is first.

Words that are rare in your notes count for more than words that are everywhere. A phrase you typed exactly gets a bonus. That is the whole trick — it is called TF-IDF and it is decades old.

Because of that: ask with the words you would expect to find in the answer. If you ask "what about the thing we discussed?" it can only help if your notes use those words too.

Nothing leaves this computer, and your notes are never modified — they are only read.
