Put your text files in this folder.

Any .txt, .md or .rst file works. Plain text is best: open a document, select
all of it, copy, paste into Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac), and save it
here as a .txt file.

Then go back to the page and press refresh - this folder is re-read every time
you ask a question, so there is nothing to restart.

Files whose names start with an underscore or a dot are ignored, which is why
this note never shows up in your search results. Delete it whenever you like.
